Note: the gzip -> lz4_legacy ramdisk recompression is magiskboot's own
default repack behavior, confirmed unrelated to the architecture bug above —
the same conversion happens whether the embedded binaries are the correct
32-bit ones or the incorrect 64-bit ones; lz4_legacy decoding is a standard,
widely supported kernel ramdisk format.
